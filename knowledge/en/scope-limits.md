## What PickFrame does not track yet
PickFrame does not yet track editing or the delivery of final results in other apps. Final results are handed to the client outside PickFrame, and then you mark the project as done.

## What PickFrame is not
PickFrame helps coordinate photo sessions, client selections, and project administration. PickFrame is not a replacement for photo backups, not a provider of final delivery, not a certified accounting service, and not a payment processor.

## Where source photos live
Source photos remain yours and stay on your Drive or your local device.
