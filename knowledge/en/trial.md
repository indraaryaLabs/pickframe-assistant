## What the trial includes
The trial lasts 24 hours from activation. During the trial you get up to 3 projects, 3 galleries, and 6 issued documents, including PickFrame Select. The trial needs no card and does not renew automatically.

## How to start the trial
Start the trial with the button in the workspace. The trial can be used only once. Do not create extra accounts to abuse the trial, because the terms of service forbid it.

## After the trial ends
After the trial ends, the Free plan stays active with one project, one gallery, and one new invoice every week. Your data and client links remain available.
