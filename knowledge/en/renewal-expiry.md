## How to renew a plan
To renew, buy a plan again and enter the activation code in the account menu. Renewing early adds time from the current end date of the plan, so remaining time is not lost.

## After a plan expires
After a paid plan expires, your data and client links remain available. The Free plan stays active with one project, one gallery, and one new invoice every week. Projects and client selections stay in place.

## A price rise after you have paid
A price change does not alter a period that is already paid for. A change in plan limits does not delete data or shorten the active period.
