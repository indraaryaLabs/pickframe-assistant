## The 14-day guarantee
A 14-day guarantee applies to the first paid plan purchase. If the first plan does not suit you, ask for a refund within 14 calendar days of activation. Include the account email and the payment reference with the request.

## After a refund is approved
If a refund is approved, paid access ends and the account returns to no active plan. Data is not deleted and can still be viewed and exported.

## Limits of the guarantee
The 14-day guarantee does not apply again on renewals. The guarantee covers only the first paid plan purchase.
