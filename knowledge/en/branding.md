## Studio name on galleries and documents
The Pro and Studio plans show your studio name in the gallery header, on invoices and receipts, and as one credit line in the gallery footer. The Free plan does not show a studio name. The studio name also appears during the trial.

## The PickFrame brand stays
Paid plans do not remove the PickFrame brand. A small Powered by PickFrame attribution stays visible on galleries and documents, next to your studio name.
