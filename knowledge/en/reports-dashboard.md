## Revenue dashboard
Once service package prices are set, the dashboard answers how much came in this month, how much is unpaid, and which orders are running. An event calendar and client history are also available.

## Reports and Excel export
The Reports menu holds business figures and access to all documents. Exporting a new Excel workbook is available on every plan. Updating an old Excel workbook without losing records is only available on the Studio plan.

## Views, language, and theme
The workspace has three views. The language can be switched between Indonesian and English, and the theme can be set to light, dark, or follow the system.
