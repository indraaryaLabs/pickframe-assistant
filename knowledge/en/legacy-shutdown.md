## Old lifetime codes
An old lifetime code is valid for one photographer for as long as the service operates. It cannot be transferred or resold, and it does not cover features that are later sold separately.

## If the service is discontinued
If the service is discontinued, PickFrame gives at least 30 days of notice to export data and refunds unused paid time proportionally. For refund calculation only, an old lifetime plan is treated as a 5-year prepayment from the purchase date. After 5 years no remainder is refunded.
