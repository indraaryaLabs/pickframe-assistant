## Account and credentials
One account is for one user only, so do not share credentials. Keep your password and client links safe. Contact the PickFrame team right away if you suspect unauthorized access.

## Who can open a link
Anyone holding a gallery link can open the gallery and send a selection without an account. Anyone holding a document link can read the document.

## Data ownership
Photos and business records remain yours. You must have the right to share the photos and to store client data.

## Corrections and data deletion
Questions, corrections, or data deletion requests are sent to the contact listed in the terms of service.
