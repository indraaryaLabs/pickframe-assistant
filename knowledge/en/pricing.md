## Pro plan prices
The Pro plan costs Rp 69,000 for 1 month, Rp 189,000 for 3 months (Rp 63,000 per month), and Rp 690,000 for 12 months (Rp 57,500 per month). The 12-month option is the best value.

## Studio plan prices
The Studio plan costs Rp 99,000 for 1 month, Rp 269,000 for 3 months (about Rp 89,667 per month), and Rp 990,000 for 12 months (Rp 82,500 per month). The 12-month option is the best value.

## Free plan price
The Free plan costs nothing, with no subscription fee. PickFrame does not store payment cards.

## Currency and price changes
All PickFrame prices are in rupiah. A price change does not alter a period that is already paid for.
