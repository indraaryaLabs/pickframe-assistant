## Document types
PickFrame creates down payment invoices, settlement invoices, and receipts. Document numbers run in sequence automatically, there are three layout choices, and the client receives a link that can be opened or printed. Documents can be exported as PDF, PNG, or JPG.

## Payments do not go through PickFrame
PickFrame records payments on a project and does not receive or move money. PickFrame is not a payment processor and not a certified accounting service.

## Document links and WhatsApp
Anyone holding a document link can read it. Deleting a document turns off its link but does not delete the payment recorded on the project. The WhatsApp button opens a message containing the document link for you to review and send yourself, while automatic PDF attachments would need another integration.

## Documents as a personal archive
A document may be kept as a personal archive. Creating a link and sending it to the client is your choice.
