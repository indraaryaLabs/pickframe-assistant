## Free plan limits
The Free plan includes 1 project per week, 1 new gallery per week, and 1 invoice or receipt per week. A Free gallery holds up to 100 photos, and PickFrame stores up to 10,000 photo references.

## Pro plan limits
The Pro plan includes 50 projects per month, 50 new galleries per month, and 250 invoices or receipts per month. A Pro gallery holds up to 1,500 photos, and PickFrame stores up to 10,000 photo references.

## Studio plan limits
The Studio plan includes 250 projects per month, 250 new galleries per month, and 1,250 invoices or receipts per month. A Studio gallery holds up to 3,000 photos, and PickFrame stores up to 30,000 photo references.

## Customers on older plans
Older Pro and Studio accounts use the same plan limits shown on the Pricing page. A change in limits does not delete data and does not shorten the active period.
