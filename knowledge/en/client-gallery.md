## How client galleries work
Paste a Google Drive folder link and send one gallery link back to the client. The client chooses photos without creating an account, and what you receive is file names, not a list of numbers over WhatsApp. Anyone holding the gallery link can open the gallery and send a selection without an account.

## Drive folders and ZIP downloads
A gallery uses an existing Drive folder, and its previews are loaded from Google. When the client downloads a ZIP, the selected files are copied into the download archive. The optional ZIP can keep a temporary copy.

## Sharing a Drive folder
Sharing a Drive folder as Anyone with the link is your decision, and anyone who has the Drive link can access it. Drive use also follows Google's terms.

## What a client selection means
A client selection means the photos are ready for editing, not that the job is finished.
