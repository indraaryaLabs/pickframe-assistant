## Three plans
PickFrame has three plans: Free, Pro, and Studio. The Free plan costs nothing and stays active with one project, one gallery, and one new invoice every week. The Pro plan is for independent photographers. The Studio plan is for vendors and agencies who handle many events at once.

## Feature differences between plans
PickFrame Select is available on the Pro plan, the Studio plan, and during the trial, but not on the Free plan. Updating an old Excel workbook is only available on the Studio plan. The studio name on galleries and documents only appears on paid plans and during the trial.

## Features included in every plan
Every plan includes a catalog of service packages with prices, a dashboard of revenue, unpaid invoices, and running orders, an event calendar, client history, new Excel workbook export, three workspace views, Indonesian and English, and light, dark, or system themes. Projects and client selections stay in place when a plan runs out.
